# Workspace tab organisation

Folders organise tabs within workspaces. A tab entry and its loaded page are distinct: unloading the page does not remove the entry.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A browsing context with its own collection of tabs and folder tree.

**Folder**:
A named container for tabs and subfolders within one workspace.

**Folder tab**:
A tab assigned to a folder. Its entry can remain in that folder while its page is unloaded.

**Unfiled tab**:
A tab not assigned to any folder. It retains ordinary Vivaldi close behaviour.

**Loaded tab**:
A tab whose page is loaded.

**Unloaded tab**:
A tab whose page is not loaded but whose entry remains available to open again. It may have been unloaded deliberately or automatically.

**Unload**:
Release a tab's loaded page while retaining its entry and place in its folder.
_Avoid_: Close, remove

**Close**:
Remove a tab entry and its associated page, if loaded.
_Avoid_: Unload
